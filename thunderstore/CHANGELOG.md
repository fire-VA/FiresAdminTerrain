* v0.2.488 - one zone at a time worlds, rivers and lakes, and the admin tools move to the hammer
  - new "one zone at a time" worlds: start on a patch of land in an endless sea, feed Chunk Generators for Chunk Seeds, and plant them with the Chunk Hammer to open the world square by square (experimental)
  - every one-zone world starts beside a rocky hill with a walk-in cave, where the first Chunk Hammer waits in a chest that brings a new one the day after it is taken
  - right-click a world on the main menu to clone it as a one-zone, full voxel, voxel wrap or heightmap world, choosing its sky islands, rivers and caves; the original is never touched (also fires_world_clone)
  - a world converted to one zone at a time keeps everything in its explored zones and brings it back as each square is opened; the Eye of the Beholder lets you raise different land there instead
  - Hugin explains the world, the Chunk Hammer and the Chunk Generator the first time you meet each
  - each seed raises its own biome, the forge turns seeds into dungeons, boss altars, landmarks and one-of-a-kind places, and boss altars and key sites rise on their own when you come near
  - skylands with their own biomes and weather float above one-zone worlds, from small islets to great skylands, with rare chains and atolls; the bigger ones carry hills, cliff terraces, ponds, rivers and rim falls, and caves dressed from their own biome
  - caves are dressed on dedicated servers again, with biome pickables, ore, hanging roots and monster spawners, their chests and pickables restock, and floor clutter comes in scattered patches
  - the New World screen has rows under Seed for the terrain generator, terrain, one zone at a time, sky islands, rivers and lakes and caves; each new world keeps those choices, so its name no longer decides how it is generated
  - rivers, lakes and ponds carved into existing worlds, with beaches, reeds and lilies
  - water that steps down (a pond into a lake, a channel to a lower reach, a sheet over an edge) pours over as a waterfall shaped to the ground below
  - pickaxes dig real tunnels, and wards and no-build areas are respected
  - admin terrain tools moved to the hammer (Misc tab), plus new Vriver, Vpour and Vpool tools
  - terrain, water, ice and snow editing commands now need admin (devcommands), and the server rejects water edits from non-admins
  - sky islands saved with the world, config presets, and a new inland water look
  - faster joins on dedicated servers: the map is kept between joins and the server's baked terrain downloads once
  - fixed most of the mod not loading on clients since Valheim 1.0, missing grass, lost terrain paint, buried or flooded start temples and a short freeze about once a minute while exploring
  - Terrain Mode now defaults to Off, so check yours after updating (see the README)
